Live Meeting Companion — How It Works

Even Realities G2 Glasses × Salesforce × AI

🥽 You & Your Glasses
🤖 AI Behind the Scenes
☁️ Salesforce
① Before the meeting
👓
Open the app on your glasses
You see a list of your Salesforce accounts. Tap to open one.
📋
Account data is fetched
Pipeline value, open deals, contacts, and last interaction are pulled in real time.
↓
↓
📊
Review account on the glasses
Pipeline chart, contacts, and AI-generated talking points appear on the display.
✍️
Talking points are generated
AI reads the account's deals and history, then writes 3–5 focused talking points for you.
② Starting the meeting
✌️
Double-tap to start
The display switches to meeting mode. The mic turns on. A timer starts.
🎯
First cues appear instantly
Your 3 best talking points are shown as opening cues — no speech needed yet.
📝
Prep record is loaded
The account's Meeting Prep record is fetched to anchor the AI's suggestions throughout.
③ During the meeting (repeats every ~5 seconds)
🗣️
You speak naturally
Hold the conversation as you normally would. The glasses listen silently in the background.
↓
🔄
Glasses show ● REC ↻
A brief spinner appears while your last few seconds of speech are being processed.
👂
Speech is transcribed
The audio clip is converted to text using OpenAI Whisper — fast, accurate, cloud-based.
↓
💡
New cues appear on the display
The glasses show 2–3 updated suggestions — specific to what was just said in the room.
🧠
Cues are generated
AI combines what was just said + the full conversation so far + your prep points to write targeted next-step cues.
④ Ending the meeting
☝️
Single-tap to end & save
The mic turns off. The glasses show "Summarising & saving…"
📄
Summary is written
AI reads the full conversation transcript and produces a summary, action items, and next steps.
↓
💾
Meeting note saved automatically
A Meeting Note record is created on the account — with the summary, action items, next steps, and timestamp. No typing required.
↓
✅
Summary on the glasses
You see the summary, action items, and "Saved to Salesforce ✓" — then tap back to your accounts list.
